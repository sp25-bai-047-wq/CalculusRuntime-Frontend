## Exports and their topics (subject: Multivariable Calculus — the Lagrange Multipliers guide)
Each export holds **exactly 15 questions with exactly 3 options each**. The eight banks sit in eight different sections of the same guide, so keep each bank clearly focused on its own slice and do not repeat ideas between banks.
- `LAGRANGE_GEOMETRY_QUIZ` — the geometric core: level curves of the objective tangent to the constraint curve at an optimum, gradients parallel at tangency, why $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$ geometrically, what happens when gradients are not parallel, reading a contour picture.
- `LAGRANGE_MATH_QUIZ` — the mathematical derivation and structure: the Lagrangian $\mathcal L=f-\lambda g$, the full system $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g$ together with $g=k$, counting equations vs unknowns, the regularity condition $\nabla g\ne\mathbf 0$, the sign/meaning of $\lambda$.
- `LAGRANGE_FIELDS_QUIZ` — separating objective from constraint: given a word problem, identify which function is $f$ and which is $g=k$; write the constraint in the form $g(x,y)=k$; compute $\nabla f$ and $\nabla g$ for concrete functions; spot a mis-stated constraint.
- `LAGRANGE_CALC_QUIZ` — single-constraint computational workflow: fully solve concrete problems, e.g. extremize $f(x,y)=xy$ on $x^2+y^2=8$, $f=x+2y$ on $x^2+y^2=5$, minimize distance to a line/plane, maximize a box volume with fixed surface area. Ask for the optimal value, the optimal point, or the value of $\lambda$.
- `LAGRANGE_MULTI_QUIZ` — multiple constraints: $\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g+\mu\nabla h$, optimizing on the intersection curve of two surfaces, how many equations the system has, concrete two-constraint problems such as extremizing a linear function on the intersection of a plane and a sphere or a plane and a cylinder.
- `LAGRANGE_VERIFY_QUIZ` — verifying and classifying results: comparing candidate values to decide max vs min, why the second-derivative test does not directly apply, checking that the constraint is satisfied, the role of compactness (Extreme Value Theorem) in guaranteeing extrema exist, handling unbounded constraint sets, endpoint/boundary candidates.
- `LAGRANGE_INDUSTRY_QUIZ` — applications in physics and economics: utility maximization subject to a budget constraint, Cobb-Douglas production with a cost constraint, minimizing surface area (material) for a fixed volume, least-cost input mixes, entropy maximization, shadow-price problems phrased in plain words (write money amounts as "40 dollars", never with a dollar sign).
- `LAGRANGE_CHALLENGE_QUIZ` — harder mixed practice: sensitivity interpretation $\lambda = df^*/dk$, inequality constraints becoming active, degenerate cases where $\nabla g=\mathbf 0$, three-variable problems, problems with symmetry that collapse the algebra, and problems mixing Lagrange with the tangent-plane picture.
